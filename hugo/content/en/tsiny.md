This is not a quote. Public guidelines are there so you can understand the budget order without extra emails and quickly move to a precise estimate.

## How I calculate the cost

- I look at the number of systems and channels.
- I assess the depth of integrations, webhooks, and exception-handling logic.
- I check data quality, security, hosting, and access requirements.
- I account for APIs, servers, and subscriptions separately if they are not included in the estimate.

## Post-launch support

If the project is already live, support packages are available - minor fixes, monitoring, and targeted improvements without restarting the entire workflow.

## Not sure where to start?

If it is unclear what to automate first and what it would cost, start with the [express automation audit](/en/automation-audit/): UAH 2,000, 3 working days, a written report. If you order a project within 30 days after the audit, the amount is credited to its cost.
