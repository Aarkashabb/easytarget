The public prices below are guidelines, not a quote. I estimate an AI project after a short diagnostic: I look at the number of systems, the depth of integrations, data quality, security, and infrastructure.

## When AI automation makes sense

- You need to automate a single AI task without building a large platform.
- You need an AI/RAG agent that works with documents, a knowledge base, and your CRM.
- You need a voice AI agent for screening, confirmations, or first contact.

## What a project usually includes

- Designing the scenario and defining the limits of what the AI is responsible for.
- Connecting to n8n, Make, your CRM, or another system.
- Testing, basic documentation, and short support after launch.

## What is paid separately

- APIs, servers, subscriptions, STT/TTS, and other infrastructure.
- Additional integrations, new communication channels, and major scenario changes.
- Advanced requirements for security, access control, and logging.
