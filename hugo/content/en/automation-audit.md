## Who it is for

Small and mid-size businesses where the team moves data by hand every day between CRM, spreadsheets, messengers and reports, and it is unclear what to automate first.

## How it works

1. **A call of up to 30 minutes.** We go through your task and decide whether an audit is needed.
2. **50% prepayment.** I will send the bank account details once we agree on the audit.
3. **Materials from you.** You send a description of the process and examples (screenshots, spreadsheets). If access to systems is needed, it is read-only.
4. **Analysis.** I analyse the process, about 3 hours of work.
5. **Report within 3 working days** after I receive the materials. We then briefly discuss it together.

## What you get

A 2–3 page document:

- the top 3 automation candidates, prioritised;
- an estimate of how many hours per month could be saved (an estimate based on your data, not a guarantee);
- a launch price range.

For reference on typical project costs, see the [pricing page](/en/tsiny/).

## FAQ

### How is the audit different from a call of up to 30 minutes?

The call helps understand your task and whether an audit fits. The audit is a written review with priorities and an estimate of hours.

### Do you guarantee savings?

No. The estimate is based on your data and the process as described, and the actual result depends on what is implemented and how.

### What if I do not want to order a project after the audit?

The report stays with you. You can take it to any contractor or automate on your own.

### How is the audit fee credited?

If you order a project within 30 days after the audit, the UAH 2,000 is credited to its cost.

### What access is needed?

A description of the process and screenshots are often enough. If access to systems is needed, it is read-only.
