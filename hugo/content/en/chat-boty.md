Public price guidelines help you gauge the order of the budget quickly. The final estimate depends on channels, integrations, moderation, escalation logic, and security requirements.

## When you need a chatbot

- You need to receive and route leads without manual handling.
- You need to write leads to a CRM, set tags, and track statuses.
- You need an AI chatbot for business with knowledge base search and escalation to a manager (AI/RAG).

## What a project usually includes

- A Telegram, Viber, or other messenger flow.
- CRM integration and basic notifications.
- Scenario testing and short support after launch.

## What is billed separately

- Paid connectors, SMS, third-party APIs, and hosting.
- Additional channels, new scenario branches, and complex routing rules.
- A vector knowledge base and extended security requirements.
