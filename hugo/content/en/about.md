## Who I am and what I do

My name is Ivan Blagoveshchenskyi. I have been automating business processes since 2021: I have used Make since 2021 and n8n for about a year. EasyTarget is my automation service for small and mid-sized businesses, based in Ukraine. I replace manual, repetitive work with automated workflows: I connect CRM, messengers, spreadsheets, ad accounts and online stores so data no longer has to be moved by hand.

By my own estimate, the automations I have built have saved clients more than 5,000 hours of work in total.

## What I do

- **Process automation** on n8n, Make and Zapier: price guides are on the [pricing page](/en/tsiny/).
- **CRM integration:** leads, statuses, orders and customers sync without manual entry.
- **[Chatbots](/en/chat-boty/)** in Telegram and Viber for taking and routing leads.
- **[AI automation and AI agents](/en/ai-avtomatyzaciya/)** on OpenAI, Claude and Gemini, including answers from a company's knowledge base (RAG).
- **Automated reporting:** data from CRM, spreadsheets and ad accounts delivered to Telegram, a spreadsheet or a presentation.
- **[Express automation audit](/en/automation-audit/):** a written report in 3 working days when it is unclear where to start.

## Stack

- **Automation platforms:** n8n (cloud and self-hosted), Make, Zapier.
- **AI:** OpenAI API, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, RAG.
- **Code and infrastructure:** JavaScript (Node.js), Python, Docker, PostgreSQL.
- **Integrations:** Google Sheets, Telegram, Viber, Facebook Graph API, Monobank API, Manychat, CRM via API and webhook.

## How I work

1. **A call of up to 30 minutes.** We go through the task and decide whether there is something to automate.
2. **Discovery.** I look at how the process works today and propose a solution. After that the price is, as a rule, fixed up front.
3. **Build and testing.** A typical automation goes live in 2–6 weeks depending on scope.
4. **Launch and 30 days of support.** Monthly support afterwards if needed.
