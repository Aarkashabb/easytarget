AI integration for business means connecting a language model (OpenAI, Claude, Gemini) to your working systems so it takes over a specific routine task: answering questions from documents, making a first-contact call, sorting incoming requests. It is not a separate chat for experiments but a step inside a process: data arrives, the model handles it, and the result lands in your CRM, spreadsheet or Telegram.

I build these solutions on n8n and Make for small and medium businesses. Below: where AI actually works, what has already been built, what it costs and where to start.

## Where AI delivers results

**Answers from internal documents.** Employees stop searching folders for instructions and ask in Telegram instead. The bot finds the answer in company documents and links to the original. In the [case study of an AI assistant for company regulations](/en/portfolio/telegram-rag-document-assistant/), 60 people use it.

**Calls a person used to make.** A voice agent calls, runs the screening and writes the result to the CRM. In the [case study of a voice agent for candidates](/en/portfolio/voice-ai-candidate-screening/), the first three weeks saw 209 call attempts and 42 invitations from the agent.

**First-pass handling of requests.** The model identifies the request type, extracts the point and passes it to a manager already sorted: with a category and context rather than a raw email.

**Data between systems.** This is not always AI, but it is often where the savings begin: a daily transfer of orders or reports without manual entry. Examples: [SadiControl → KeyCRM](/en/portfolio/sadicontrol-keycrm-sync/) (over 500 visits per month) and [advertising → sales by product](/en/portfolio/marketing-budget-automation/).

## When you do not need AI

If a task can be solved with a simple rule ("if the amount is above X, send it here"), a model only adds cost and unpredictability. AI makes sense where there is free text, documents, speech or a decision that depends on meaning. If it becomes clear after a conversation that ordinary automation without AI is enough for you, I will say so.

## FAQ

### How much does AI integration for a business cost?

From $200 for a standalone AI task to $1,200–3,000 for an agent with a knowledge base or a voice agent. The exact amount depends on the number of systems, data quality and security requirements, and is known after a short diagnostic or an express audit.

### Is this suitable for a small business?

Yes, my main client is a small or medium business. It is better to start with one task rather than "AI for everything".

### Which models do you use?

OpenAI, Claude and Gemini, depending on the task, cost and data requirements. I choose the model for the scenario, not the other way around.

### What about data security?

Before connecting anything, we decide which data may be sent to an external model provider and which may not. Data-handling terms depend on the provider and plan, so I check them separately for each project.

### How quickly will there be a result?

A standalone task: 1–3 days. An agent: 1–3 weeks (voice up to 4). First usage figures appear in the first weeks after launch.
